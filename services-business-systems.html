Business systems

Systems placed where effort is currently performing a system's function.

Most small companies are held together by one person remembering things. The numbers live in three places, the follow-ups depend on who has time that week, and month-end takes a weekend.

We look at how the work actually happens, rather than how it's supposed to, and put systems into the places where effort is doing a system's job, usually the parts nobody enjoys.

What it looks like

  • Your numbers gathered in one place, current, without anyone assembling them
  • Follow-ups that happen on a schedule instead of when somebody remembers
  • Reporting that produces itself, in language you can hand to someone else
  • The recurring admin handled, with a record of what was done and when
  • A weekly summary of what the systems did, in plain English
Who it’s for

Businesses where the owner or one key person has become the bottleneck, and where the work is real but the way it runs hasn't caught up.

How it starts

A conversation. We ask what your week actually looks like and where things fall through, and we tell you plainly whether there is anything here worth doing.
